## 0B.1 What the source pack is and what it is telling us

The 38 documents form one engineering package for the AI Growth Engine. Documents 01 (Business Requirements) and 02 (Functional Specification) are byte-identical to the documents the blueprint was originally built from. Documents 03–36 are domain views of the same product. Each repeats a **common core**: purpose and boundary, Website/Admin operating contract, implementation acceptance controls, optimization engineering and current multi-startup scope. Each then adds its domain specifics: personas, architecture, workflows, UI/API/table catalogues, market and sales playbooks, AI engineering, plugin and cost decisions, security, testing, QC, traceability, DevOps, operations, training, SaaS service and optimization. Document 37 is a worked end-to-end example; document 38 is the authoritative inventory of counts and targets.

**Pattern and intent** (the expert reading):

1. **Fail-closed, evidence-based delivery.** Every capability is specified with its acceptance evidence. Nothing is "done" without a recorded, independently reviewed result, and synthetic or offline checks never count as production certification.
2. **Strict isolation.** The isolation is between Website and Admin, owned portfolio and external clients, and tenants and startups. Shared identity is never shared access.
3. **Configuration, not code.** New startups and clients are onboarded through templates, capability profiles and governed configuration revisions, while running work stays pinned to its revision.
4. **Controlled autonomy.** AI prepares and recommends. Deterministic policy, budget and human approval decide anything consequential. Duplicate and uncertain outcomes are reconciled, never blindly retried.
5. **Open-source first, paid by exception.** Every paid plugin or provider is benchmarked, budgeted and approved, and developer tools are never runtime dependencies.
6. **Measured outcomes.** 35 optimization KPIs (21 mandatory), 104 optimization controls and 24 scenarios measure security, cost, automation, reliability and conversion. They are referred to as **OKPI-01…35** in this blueprint to avoid clashing with business KPIs.
