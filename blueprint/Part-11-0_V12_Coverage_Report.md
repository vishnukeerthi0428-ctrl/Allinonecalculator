## 11.0.1 What the revision is

V12 is a new, much larger specification of the same product. Only about 3% of the BRD's and 11% of the FSD's normative text overlaps with the original 38-document pack. It turns the platform into a SaaS catalogue of **39 automation services (SM-SVC-01…39), each with four executable workflows (156 workflows, `SM-SVC-##-WF#`)**, sold per service through a subscription, bundle and entitlement engine, with billing and metering, a service registry, platform operator administration and an authorized portfolio command centre. It adds voice and telephony, SMS/RCS, agentic commerce, marketplace and procurement services, enterprise readiness (SSO/SCIM, SLA tiers, certifications, 24×7 operations), India/EU residency and global expansion. The historical annexes are the V1 baseline, declared non-normative; the embedded archive includes the original 38 documents (byte-identical to the pack already covered) and a separate "Investment Automation" product.
