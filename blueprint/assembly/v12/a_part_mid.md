
**Reading the register.** Partially covered, Not covered and Conflict aspects are listed one per row with the V12 wording abridged to 150 characters (the CSV holds 300). Covered and Not applicable aspects are grouped per chapter with their blueprint locations or reasons. "New IDs" point to the enhancements in Part 11.1b §11.1.5 (FR-V12-A-###), the decisions in §11.1.7 (DEC-500…519), the ADRs (ADR-180…184) and the tags in §11.1.9. A row ends **Matched** when the enhancement or decision closes it; **Pending Founder decision** when it meets a Founder decision or a safety invariant.
